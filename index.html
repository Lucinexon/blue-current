The signals

Six pressures. One connected ocean.

These problems rarely happen alone. Climate stress can weaken reefs; pollution can compound habitat loss; and the effects travel through food webs and coastal communities.

Room to recover

Protection works.
Scale is the challenge.

Well-designed marine protected areas can give habitats and wildlife space to recover, especially when protections are enforced and local communities help shape them. The goal is not just lines on a map; it is meaningful protection in the water.
